## What the change does

Mode 300's noise-injection window started at 7800 µs, 13 µs before the noise diode switches on (about 7813 µs), so it took in the diode's switch-on burst: MISA's mode-300 T_sys was 26 % too low on the benchmark and its range-Doppler densities too low by 1.29-1.37 (Memo 27); over the whole of 8 April the factor is 1.24-1.38 and varies through the day (gate B). The window started at 7830 µs at gate A and starts at 7850 µs since `065049d` (`radar_timing.TMM[300]["noise0"]`), where the range-Doppler path's filter has let the burst go.

- **Findings and what was done:**
  - **E1, the site log reads MISA at 0.96 of ours after the fix, not 1.** On 40 periods of 8 April, the site's MISA T_sys over ours (recomputed from raw) is 1.278 with the window at 7800 (95 % hour-block bootstrap range 1.240-1.298), 0.960 at 7830 (0.954-0.964) and 0.953 at 7850. This is the absolute MISA scale at the 4-5 % level. *Open question* (for Juha): how the site computes its real-time T_sys (its windows, and whether it uses a diode temperature per antenna); for zenith's day/night difference, whether there is ionospheric echo in our background window at 1080-1155 km range (the reviewer's estimate, not measured).
  - **E2, this record's explanation of the lost debris detection was wrong.** `fit_lp.py:521` forms LPA = RDS_LP × α, and RDS_LP was stored as raw power divided by α, so α cancels exactly: LPA differs between the runs by at most 4e-16 relative. Re-running main's `fit_lp.fit_gaussian` on both runs' spectra reproduces 12 against 11 detections; the lost one is at 589 km in `il_1712592160`, at a power-to-noise ratio of 3.15-3.3 (threshold 0.5).   - **E3, the size of the effect was understated: about +34 % over the day, not +26-30 %.** On 40 periods over 8 April the correction factor (new T_sys over old) is 1.24-1.38, median 1.34: 1.31-1.38 before about 15 UT, 1.24-1.29 from 15:30 UT on, through the eclipse (correlation with time −0.70). So the old MISA range-Doppler densities carried a bias that changed by about 8 % during the eclipse day, which matters for any MISA eclipse time series made from the old products. *Done:* the range and its time dependence are in the code comment (`065049d`), this record and Memo 27.
