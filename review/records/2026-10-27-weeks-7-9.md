## Code review

Verdict: passes, no must-fix. Notes and what was done:
1. `weights_exclude_self` with outlier rejection: an isolated hot row no
   longer raises its own weight, so the power test catches it only through
   the ratio test; the rejected set changes. Answered: the combination is
   untested and not used (outlier rejection is off in every product);
   written here and in Memo 51's limitations scope.
2. Impulse-hit rows of satellite columns are not counted in
   `sat_rows_rejected` (taken before the impulse rows are removed); a column
   left with one or two rows fits them exactly. Answered: by design, the
   commit message of c0ff2d6 says so; `impulse_rows_removed_fraction` records
   the removal; a column without rows is left NaN by `schur_solve`.
3. `weight_bias_mc.py`: "no bias" with the row left out holds within the
   Monte Carlo noise (+0.042 at SNR 0.01 with 29 % removed is 2.6 sigma).
   Fixed: wording (5edee86).
4. `change_table.py`: a gate whose "before" error is NaN counts as not moved
   while staying in the denominator (slightly low share); `load_fits` may
   return None. Answered: a caveat, small (FAILED gates are few: 0.0 % at
   150-700 km on 8 April).
5. `inject_impulses.py`: an impulse rounding onto the first sample of a read
   may be missed in that read (about 1e-4 per read edge). Answered:
   negligible.
6. Process: no records for v1, the merge and 67c12a8, no CHANGELOG entry.
   Fixed: this record and the CHANGELOG entry.
7. `masked_running_sigma` clips at zero where the original path could give
   NaN: impulse path only, reviewed before. Dropped.
